Property Overview

Sophisticated City Living
at BLU

Experience sophisticated city living at BLU, a striking 21-story luxury condominium tower in the heart of San Francisco’s vibrant Yerba Buena neighborhood. Perched above a lush green treeline, this beautifully appointed 2-bedroom, 2-bath residence showcases captivating city views, dramatic floor-to-ceiling windows, and abundant natural light.

The open-concept living and dining areas flow seamlessly into a chef’s kitchen featuring granite countertops and premium Viking and Bosch appliances.

The spacious primary suite offers a custom-organized walk-in closet, spa-inspired bath and state-of-the-art window treatments. Thoughtful upgrades include luxury vinyl plank flooring and designer lighting. Additional conveniences include central heating and air conditioning, in-unit laundry, and one-car secured parking.

Residents enjoy a 24-hour attended lobby, beautifully landscaped courtyard with BBQ and outdoor seating, and secure bike storage.

An exceptional location places SFMOMA, Salesforce Park, Union Square, the Embarcadero, world-class dining, shopping, and entertainment just moments away, with convenient access to BART, Caltrain, and major freeways.

Discover the perfect balance of sophisticated design, modern comfort, and exceptional San Francisco living at BLU.
